# Road to Full Time — the $200k exit plan

*Written Oct 2026, after the Choir rename. The master plan still governs Year 1 (Nov 2026 – Oct 2027);
this doc governs Year 2+ and the quit decision. Companion docs: side-hustle-master-plan.md, choir-suite-plan.md.*

## 1. The bet

Each shipped product teaches exactly one skill:

1. **IA Helper** — how to ship (stores, releases, support queues).
2. **MediaMaid** — how to monetize when the API and code are mostly static.
3. **Choir** — how to scale, and how to run recurring revenue in an existing market,
   without custom assets and without creating a market from zero.

The 4th step is **not a new product — it is a mode shift**: from shipping products to running
the business the products created. Solo, that business has three layers:

- **Software sales** (one-time, the current plan) — funds the launches.
- **Choir Managed** (recurring) — the floor that covers fixed costs.
- **Premium commissions** (services) — the ceiling that crosses the quit threshold.

## 2. The honest math

- Salary to beat: $200k + benefits ≈ **$215k total comp**. Solo life also adds self-employment
  tax and health insurance (~$15–25k/yr, US) and drops the 401k match.
- **You do not need $215k from products to quit.** You need the floor + trajectory + runway:
  - Trailing 12-month income ≥ **$100k**, of which **≥ 50% is recurring**;
  - **18–24 months of expenses** banked;
  - Health insurance quotes solved 6 months before the date;
  - The marginal-hour test: the next hour of Carl-time earns clearly more in the business
    than the job's effective rate. When that is true for a full quarter, the job is the drag.

## 4. The 4th step: Choir Live

**Recommendation:** the managed-show business on top of the Choir suite.

- **Choir Managed fleet** — $19–25/mo per server. Not commodity hosting ($3–8/mo floor); the product
  is "you never touch plugin config again, ever." Annual TShock breakage is absorbed by us, on
  schedule. Mini-PC hardware we own (Terraria server binaries are x64 — not Pi); capped cohorts;
  best-effort SLA in writing; freeze signups the quarter ops bust 2 hrs/wk.
- **Premium commissions** — custom show builds for large communities and streamers, $5–15k each,
  60-day delivery. The adjacent Minecraft market already pays for custom plugin work (r/admincraft
  hire threads; professional commission shops such as tux2mc.com) — Terraria's equivalent is smaller
  and unserved. The Choir show is the funnel: every episode is a demo of exactly what a commission buys.
- **The productization rule — no hour without an asset.** Every commission must collapse into a
  reusable config pack, mode, or engine feature within 60 days of delivery. Services pay the bills;
  the asset they leave behind is the compounding part. Billable Carl is the bridge, never the end state.

**Fallback 4th step** (if commissions have not materialized by Apr 2029): institutional licensing of
IA Helper / MediaMaid — libraries, researchers, studios; $99–299/site licenses, few big tickets,
no new product surface. Decide at the F3 review, not before.

## 5. Anti-race-to-zero rules (pricing power)

1. **Never discount.** Founding prices rise on the published schedule. Sales add value; they never cut price.
2. **Price against the incumbent's real cost** — hassle, hours, trust — never against free or the commodity floor.
3. **Recurring stays metered to real per-user cost** (rule-2 discipline). Metered recurring is honest,
   and honesty is churn control — which is the actual anti-zero defense.
4. **Build switching costs deliberately:** persistent state (Chronical lineage, world history), hosted
   configs, the operator relationship. Data gravity is the moat. Commodity hosts cannot follow us there.
5. **Never invert the layers:** one-time sales fund launches; recurring carries the floor; services
   provide the ceiling. Do not fund recurring promises with one-time cash (no lifetime subscriptions).

## 7. Risk register

- **Terraria ecosystem decay** (Terraria 2, someday): the transferable assets are the blocks, the
  ops discipline, and the commission relationships — they move to any game-server ecosystem.
- **Ops pages:** capped cohorts, best-effort SLA, the freeze rule. Sleep is a feature, not a luxury.
- **Insurance / self-employment tax:** budget $20–25k/yr inside the quit math; solve quotes early.
- **Solo energy:** the show must stay fun. If the show becomes a chore, the marketing engine dies with it.
- **Commission concentration:** no single client above 25% of income.
- **Thinking small:** the counter-risk. The gates exist so the leap is measured, not so it never happens.
